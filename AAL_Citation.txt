Project Context & Template Details:
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Template: Automated Anatomical Labeling (AAL) - Original Version (v1)
- Reference Space: MNI (Montreal Neurological Institute) single-subject brain
- Structure: 90 cerebral regions (cortical and subcortical) and 26 cerebellar regions
- Application: This atlas serves as the primary anatomical reference space for 
  surface mesh extraction, co-registration alignment, and regional labeling 
  of statistical shape variations.
